**NHSE — Animal Crossing: New Horizons save editor for Switch 2**

Edit your island directly from the homebrew app: `nhse.nro`.

**Saves & backups**
• Open the game's save directly and choose your console user.
• Open a save folder, `main.dat`, or `.zip` from the SD card.
• Automatic backups when opening saves.
• Browse, open, restore, or delete backups.
• Save changes, discard edits, and check save hashes.

**Players & inventory**
• Edit wallet, bank savings, current/total Nook Miles, hotel tickets, and Poki.
• Edit pockets and home storage.
• Search items by name or ID; edit quantity, color variant, and raw value.
• Copy, paste, and clear item slots.
• Learn all supported DIY recipes and reactions, and complete the catalog.

**Island**
• Edit Sunday's turnip purchase price and the week's selling prices.
• Donate all missing museum fish, bugs, sea creatures, fossils, and genuine art.
• Remove weeds, branches, and shells; fill holes; water every flower.
• Empty the recycle bin.

**Villagers**
• View residents and ask them to move out or stay.
• Replace villagers using bundled templates, including their house and belongings.
• Press `X` in the villager list to replace a resident. Requires a game `2.0+` save.

**Map editor**
• Edit terrain models, variations, angles, elevation, and paths.
• Place/remove field items on either layer, with placement checks.
• Terrain preview with the selected tile marked.

**Custom designs**
• Normal, PRO, island flag, and Able Sisters designs.
• Rename designs, paint individual pixels, and edit palette colors.
• Import/export `.nhd`, `.nhpd`, and `32×32` PNG files using SD card paths.
• PRO designs support all four sheets; PNG imports requantize the shared palette.

**Houses**
• Edit player/villager exterior wall, roof, and door style IDs.
• Edit player house loans.
• Edit furniture slots in all six player rooms across eight layers.
• Edit wallpaper, flooring, and accent wallpaper, including villager house surfaces.

**Appearance**
• Light, dark, or system theme, remembered between launches.

**Controls & current limits**
• Map and house editors use numeric coordinates and game IDs.
• Terrain/exterior combinations aren't fully validated.
• Close Animal Crossing before opening its save. Changes reach the game when you save with `+`.
• New editors passed host tests and the `.nro` build; physical Switch 2 testing is still pending.
